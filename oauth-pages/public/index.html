Nest AC Monitor

A personal, single-user monitoring utility.

Nest AC Monitor is a private application that runs on its owner's own computer. It reads temperature, humidity, and operating status from the owner's own Nest thermostat through the Google Smart Device Management API, and sends the owner a notification when the readings indicate the air conditioning has stopped cooling.

What it does

  • Reads thermostat data every few minutes.
  • Records the readings to a local database on the owner's computer.
  • Notifies the owner when the thermostat reports cooling but the temperature is not falling.

What it does not do

  • It does not send any commands to the thermostat. It is read-only.
  • It has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no other users.
  • It transmits no data to this website or to any third party.

The application is not distributed and is not available for use by anyone else.
